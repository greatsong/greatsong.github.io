English

Song Sukree (송석리) is an Informatics (computer science) teacher at Danggok High School in Seoul, South Korea, and an author on data and AI education.

  • Teaching career: Sunrin Internet High School (2005–2016), Hansung Science High School (2016–2021), Seoul High School (2021–2026), Danggok High School (2026–present)
  • Lecturer at the Seoul National University College of Education and SNU Continuing Education (2020–2026)
  • Board member of Kakao Impact (2022–2024)
  • Designated "Teacher Developer No. 0" by the Seoul Metropolitan Office of Education (2025); co-organizer of its Teacher Developer Hackathons No. 1 (2025) and No. 2 (2026); organizer of the first Sunrin Internet High School hackathon (2015)
  • Military service: Republic of Korea Army, 11th Mechanized Infantry Division (2009–2011)
  • Curriculum researcher for Korea's national "AI Mathematics" (2015 revision) and "Data Science" (2022 revision) curricula; co-author of high school Informatics, AI Basics (2015 and 2022 revisions) and Data Science textbooks
  • Online courses: developer of the EBS ESOF course "Data Analysis Starter Class with Python"; instructor of the No. 1 data science course on Class101 (as "리송쌤")
  • Books include Data Analysis for Everyone with Python (Gilbut, 2019), Minimum Data Literacy (Gilbut, 2024) and Integrated Simulation Classes with VPython (Gilbut, 2025)
  • Runs "Growing Together" (함께 자라기), a national community of secondary school Informatics teachers (since 2022; 973 members as of 17 November 2026), and the teacher coding community Zzolab
